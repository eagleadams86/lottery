Tax year 2026. Federal tax is worked out on the 2026 brackets (IRS Rev. Proc. 2025-32) after the standard deduction, and New York on its 2026 brackets with the tax benefit recapture that flattens a large prize to a single rate — so a $500,000 share is taxed as $500,000, not at a jackpot’s top rate. Withholding is fixed by law and does not follow those brackets: 24% federal on any prize of $5,000 or more, and 10.9% to New York, which is the state’s highest effective rate (20 NYCRR 171.11, Pub 140-W) whatever you end up owing — which is why a smaller prize comes back as a refund at filing. NYC and Yonkers residents pay their own tax on top and none of it is withheld. Assumes the prize is your only income for the year, no itemised deductions and no credits; annuity payments are taxed on today’s brackets, since nobody knows 2055’s. Lottery winnings are ordinary income; FICA does not apply. Estimates only — consult a CPA for exact liability. Jackpot data via usamega.com. Winning numbers via data.ny.gov.

🔒 Private — everything is worked out in your browser. Nothing you type is uploaded and there is no account; the only requests are to the public jackpot, winning-number and Treasury feeds.
