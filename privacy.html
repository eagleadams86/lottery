NY Lottery Calculator & Portfolio — Privacy Policy

Effective August 18, 2026

Both pages — the after-tax calculator and the lump-sum portfolio model — run entirely in your browser. There is no account, no sign-in and no server of ours holding anything.

What These Pages Store

Only your own settings, in your browser's local storage: the theme you picked, the jackpot game and number of winners you last used, which sections you left open, and the portfolio page's slider values (take-home amount, spending, yields and the equity split). The figures you type are never sent anywhere — every calculation happens on your device. Clearing the site's browser data deletes all of it.

What Leaves Your Browser

Current jackpots. The calculator asks a small Cloudflare Worker (lottery-proxy.charlie-adams-176.workers.dev) for the current Powerball and Mega Millions jackpots. It reads them from a public lottery site and passes the two numbers back. Nothing about you or your figures is included in the request.

Winning numbers. Recent draw results come from New York State's own open-data service at data.ny.gov. Again, only a request for public results — nothing of yours goes with it.

T-bill yield. The portfolio page reads the current Treasury yield curve from home.treasury.gov so the cash bucket starts at a real rate.

Those three are the only network calls either page makes, and the pages' Content-Security-Policy blocks anything else outright. Like any web request, each one necessarily shows the service your IP address — that is all they receive.

What These Pages Do Not Do

  • No analytics or usage-tracking scripts
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No cookies, no accounts, no sign-in
  • No third-party code — the chart library is a copy served from this site, not a CDN
  • No selling or sharing data with third parties; there is nothing to sell

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
