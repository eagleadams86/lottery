NY Lottery Calculator & Portfolio

Two pages, both running entirely in your browser. No account, and nothing you type leaves your device.

Take-Home Calculator →

What a Powerball or Mega Millions win actually pays a New York State resident after federal and state tax — computed on the cash lump sum, split any number of ways. Current jackpots and the latest winning numbers load automatically.

Lump-Sum Portfolio Model →

A what-if model of investing the lump sum: how long it lasts at a given spending level, split between cash, bonds and equities, with the cash bucket starting from the current Treasury yield.
